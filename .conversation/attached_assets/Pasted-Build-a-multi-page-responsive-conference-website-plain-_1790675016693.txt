## DESIGN RULE: NO FLAT SOLID COLORS ANYWHERE
Every background must use a pattern, texture, or layered gradient, never a single flat color. Build a reusable pattern library in CSS (SVG data-URIs or inline SVG, no external image dependencies) and apply it consistently:
- .pattern-neural: nodes and connecting lines (cyan on navy), used in the hero, navbar, and footer
- .pattern-circuit: PCB-style traces with small dots, used on dark sections
- .pattern-hex: hexagon grid with a faint glow, used on card headers and date cards
- .pattern-dots: dot-matrix grid, used on light sections
- .pattern-grid: fine blueprint/tech grid lines, used on the Committee and Registration pages
- .pattern-waves: flowing data-wave lines, used as section dividers (SVG wave/diagonal clip-path between sections instead of straight edges)
- .pattern-mesh: multi-stop mesh gradient (navy, blue, cyan, violet accents) layered under any pattern
Rules: light sections use a soft off-white or pale-blue base with .pattern-dots or .pattern-grid at 4 to 8% opacity; dark sections use a navy-to-blue mesh gradient with .pattern-neural or .pattern-circuit at 10 to 20% opacity; alternate them down the page so no two neighbouring sections look identical. Add a slow background-position drift animation (60s+) to the dark patterns. Keep text contrast at WCAG AA on top of every pattern.

Palette: deep navy #050B1F, blue #0A4DFF, cyan #00E5FF, violet accent #7C3AED (used sparingly), light background tones #F4F8FF and #E8F1FF. Fonts: [Poppins or Inter] for body, [Orbitron or Space Grotesk] for headings.
